How this website uses cookies and similar technologies.

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Our use of cookies

This website does not set its own cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking technologies. For that reason, it does not show a cookie consent banner.

Third-party services

Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts and some images from Unsplash, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When your browser requests these files, the providers receive standard technical information such as your IP address. They do not set cookies through this website at the time of writing; their handling of data is described in their own privacy policies.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings. Doing so will not affect how this website works.

Changes

If we introduce cookies or analytics in the future, we will update this notice and, where required, ask for your consent first.
