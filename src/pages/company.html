About Hamerkop

Enterprise technology for institutions that need systems to work every day.

Based in Addis Ababa, Hamerkop Systems is an Ethiopian enterprise technology company helping institutions replace fragmented processes and aging technology with reliable, governed systems for day-to-day operations.

Hamerkop works across enterprise resource planning, electronic invoicing, financial technology, identity, systems integration, data and managed technology services.
