Hamerkop EIMS

Electronic invoicing that stays connected to the business behind it.

Electronic invoicing affects more than the document sent to a customer. It touches transaction data, finance, reporting, approval, validation and the systems where the original sale or service is recorded. EIMS keeps those processes connected so invoicing does not become another isolated application inside the institution.

  • Electronic invoice and receipt workflows
  • Validation and status handling
  • ERP, finance, billing and POS integration
  • Transaction traceability and audit records
  • Operational and regulatory reporting
  • Controlled roles and access
