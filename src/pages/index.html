Hamerkop EIMS

Electronic invoicing that stays connected to the business behind it.

Electronic invoicing works best when it follows the same transaction, finance and reporting flow already used by the organization.

A connected invoicing workflow

Electronic invoicing affects more than the document sent to a customer. It touches transaction data, finance, reporting, approval, validation and the systems where the original sale or service is recorded.

EIMS keeps those processes connected so invoicing does not become another isolated application inside the institution.
