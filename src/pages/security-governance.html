Company · Security, governance & compliance

Trust has to be designed into the system.

Enterprise technology carries financial records, institutional data, operational information and regulatory evidence. Control therefore sits inside architecture, workflows, access and ongoing operation.

Access control

Roles and permissions determine who can access the system and what each user is permitted to do.

Auditability

Important transactions, approvals and system activity remain traceable through audit records where required.

Integration control

System-to-system data exchange runs through defined interfaces rather than uncontrolled manual transfer.

Data governance

Data governance clarifies responsibility for how institutional information is structured, accessed and used.

Operational continuity

Support, monitoring and maintenance are aligned with the importance of the deployed system.

Regulatory alignment

Applicable reporting and operating requirements are reflected in system workflows and implementation requirements.

Technology ecosystem

Platforms we implement, customize and integrate.

Hamerkop implements, customizes and integrates enterprise technology across ERP, finance, payments, identity, databases, cloud environments, analytics and APIs. Odoo forms part of the ERP implementation capability alongside Hamerkop products and client-specific integrations.
