Legal

Privacy Policy

How Hamerkop System S.C. handles personal information shared through this website.

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Hamerkop System S.C. ("Hamerkop", "we", "us"), an enterprise technology company based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. Questions about this policy can be sent to {{site_email}}.

Information we collect

We only collect personal information that you choose to send us. When you request a consultation or demo, this may include your name, work email, organization, role or position, phone number, industry, area of interest and the project details you describe.

We do not use analytics or advertising tools on this website, and we do not build profiles of visitors.

How the consultation form works

Submitting the consultation form prepares a message to our team. Depending on how the form is configured, it either opens your own email application with the message ready to send, or delivers it through a form-processing service acting on our behalf. Either way, the information reaches us only when the request is sent.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and route it to the relevant Hamerkop team.
  • To prepare consultations, demonstrations and proposals you have asked for.
  • To keep a record of our correspondence with you.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Third-party services

To display this website, your browser connects to a small number of third-party services, which receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts, which provides the typefaces.
  • Unsplash, which provides some of the photography.

These providers process that information under their own privacy policies.

Retention

We keep enquiry correspondence for as long as needed to respond and to manage any resulting relationship, and then delete it unless we are required to keep it by law.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including Ethiopia's personal data protection legislation, you may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Contact {{site_email}} to make a request.

Security

We apply reasonable organizational and technical measures to protect the information you send us. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
