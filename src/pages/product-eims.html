Typical operating context

A transaction may begin in an ERP, POS, billing platform or another operational system. EIMS sits within that environment to manage the electronic invoice or receipt process while keeping status, reporting and traceability connected to the originating operation.

Implementation

The engagement begins with the current invoicing process, the systems that create transaction data, reporting requirements and the integration points around them. Configuration, integration, testing, rollout, training and support follow from that operating picture.

Electronic invoicing is most effective when it is part of the operation, not another system beside it.
